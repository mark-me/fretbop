# Target audience: fretted-instrument players without theory, learning alone

Fretbop teaches jazz chord progressions and soloing to players who know their instrument a little but cannot read notation and know no music theory, and who learn without a teacher. Soprano and baritone ukulele come first; guitar and bass guitar follow. This drives the didactics: sound, shapes and patterns first, theory named only after it has been played and heard, and the app must take over a teacher's feedback role.
