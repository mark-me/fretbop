# Mastery is measured by microphone and ear tests; self-assessment is a capped fallback

A step counts as mastered when microphone measurements and ear tests meet the exercise's thresholds. Self-assessment is offered only after the app has diagnosed a measurement failure (no signal, noise, clipping, out of tune, insecure connection) and can raise a step to "provisionally mastered" at most, so it cannot become a shortcut around the mastery model.
